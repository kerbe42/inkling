Handwriting fonts in Inkling: sources, credits and licences
===========================================================

Inkling draws handwritten replies stroke by stroke from single-stroke ("single-line") fonts. The font files in this
folder are the sources; device/app/gen_strokefonts.py converts them into the C tables built into the app
(device/app/stroke_fonts.c and device/app/ofl_fonts.h). The conversion keeps ASCII 32..126, flattens curves into
short lines, rescales the glyphs and adds dotless i and j. The fonts are named in the app by how they look.

1. Hershey fonts (fonts/*.jhf) - see HERSHEY-NOTICE.txt for the required acknowledgements and terms
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S. National Bureau of
Standards. The format of the font data was originally created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc.

2. EMS single-line SVG fonts (fonts/svg/EMS*.svg) - SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL.txt)

Single-line versions created by Sheldon B. Michaels, SVG font conversion by Windell H. Oskay (Evil Mad Scientist
Laboratories), from fonts licensed under the SIL Open Font License; credit and licence are embedded in each SVG
file's metadata. Source: https://gitlab.com/oskay/svg-fonts (fonts/EMS). The files here are unchanged copies.
